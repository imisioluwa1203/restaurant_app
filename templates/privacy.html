Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ACiD'S TREATS is a demonstration restaurant ordering app built as a portfolio project. Payments run in test mode and no real money is taken.

What we collect

When you sign up or log in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address. If you sign up with a form, we also store the username, phone number and password hash you provide. We store your orders (items, total, order type, status and date).

How we use it

We use your details to create your account, show your order history, send verification and password reset emails, and process test payments. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Services we use

Paystack processes payments, so we never see or store card details. Brevo sends our emails. Neon stores our database and Render hosts the site. Google and Facebook handle sign-in only.

Your choices and deleting your data

To delete your account and all data linked to it, email akinsulireimisioluwa@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete your account, profile details and order history within 7 days and confirm by email.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove ACiD'S TREATS under Facebook Settings, Apps and Websites.
